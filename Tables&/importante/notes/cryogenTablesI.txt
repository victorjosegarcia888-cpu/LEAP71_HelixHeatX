14. Sponsoring Agency Code
A 20-mm hybrid hydrostatic bearing was evaluated in liquid hydrogen at speeds to
80 000 rpm and radial loads to 440 N (100 lbf). The size and operating conditions of the bearing were selected to be compatible with the operating requirements of an advanced technology turbopump. Measured bearing stiffness was 1.5
times greater than predicted, while the fluid flow rate through the bearing was
35 to 65 percent less than predicted. lhe optimal pressure ratio for the bearing
should be between 0.2 and 0.55 depending on the balance of bearing efficiency
and stiffness. The bearing operated for 337 start-stop cycles without failure.
